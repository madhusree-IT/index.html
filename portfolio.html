Featured Projects

Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction

End-to-End

Developed a comprehensive data analytics project that analyzes customer churn patterns and predicts future churn using machine learning. Created interactive visualizations and a business intelligence dashboard to communicate findings and support customer retention strategies.

Problem: Companies often have customer data but struggle to identify why customers are leaving or which ones are at risk. This lack of insight makes it difficult to implement effective retention strategies.

Approach & Methodology

Data collection → Data cleaning & preprocessing → Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) → Feature engineering → Machine learning model training → Model evaluation → Business visualization & Dashboard creation

Key Findings: Identified critical churn patterns through EDA revealing that contract type, tenure, and monthly charges significantly influence churn behavior. Developed ML model for churn prediction and created interactive dashboard to help business prioritize customer retention efforts.
